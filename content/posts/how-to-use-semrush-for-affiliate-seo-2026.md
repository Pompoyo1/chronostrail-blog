## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### Step 1: Run a Competitor Domain Through Organic Research

Go to Semrush, click "Organic Research" in the left sidebar, and type in a competitor's domain. Pick someone ranking in your niche who clearly monetizes through affiliate links. Look at their top-performing pages sorted by traffic.

You are not looking to copy them. You are looking for the content types that earn traffic in your niche so you know where to invest your time.

### Step 2: Export Their Top Pages and Filter for Buyer Intent

Export the top pages list as a CSV. Filter it down to pages with URLs containing words like "best," "review," "vs," "alternative," or "top." These are almost always affiliate-monetized pages targeting buyers.

This single step gives you a complete map of what is working in your niche right now. I have found hundred-dollar content opportunities this way in under 20 minutes.

### Step 3: Run Each High-Traffic URL Through Keyword Gap

Take three to five competitor URLs and run them through the Keyword Gap tool. Enter your domain in the first field, then add your competitors. Set the filter to "Missing" keywords.

This shows you every keyword your competitors rank for that your site does not even appear for. That list is your content calendar for the next six months.

### Step 4: Prioritize Keywords Using KD% and Search Intent

In Semrush, sort your missing keyword list by Keyword Difficulty under 40 and search volume above 500. This combination finds keywords that are realistic to rank for without a massive backlink budget.

Check the "Intent" column. Focus on keywords marked as Transactional or Commercial Investigation. These visitors are ready to buy or compare products. Informational keywords matter too, but they convert at a fraction of the rate.

### Step 5: Build Topic Clusters Using the Keyword Magic Tool

Pick your five to ten core topics. Run each one through the Keyword Magic Tool and select "Related." Group similar keywords together into clusters.

Each cluster becomes one pillar page or one tightly focused review. This is how you build topical authority, which matters far more in 2026 than publishing isolated articles. Semrush makes clustering faster than any other tool I have used.

### Step 6: Analyze SERP Features Before Writing

Before you write anything, click on a keyword and look at the SERP Analysis section. Check what types of content are ranking. See if there are featured snippets, video results, or product carousels dominating the page.

If a comparison table appears in the featured snippet, write your article around a detailed comparison table. Matching the content format to what Google is already rewarding is not optional, it is the strategy.

### Step 7: Track Your Rankings Weekly

Add your target keywords to Semrush Position Tracking. Set the target location to match your audience geography. Check it weekly, not daily.

Daily checking creates anxiety without useful data. Weekly tracking gives you enough movement to make real decisions about whether a page needs an update or more links.

### Step 8: Use the Content Audit Tool to Fix Underperforming Pages

Connect your Google Search Console to Semrush and run a Content Audit. It pulls your existing pages and scores them against performance data. Any page sitting below position 20 with impressions but no clicks is a priority fix.

Update these pages before publishing new ones. Improving existing content is faster and cheaper than starting from scratch. I pair Semrush audit data with [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) to optimize on-page signals at the same time.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Chasing volume over intent.** A keyword with 50,000 searches per month means nothing if the searchers are not buyers. I have watched affiliates waste months writing content around high-volume informational keywords that converted at 0.1%. Buyer intent always beats raw numbers.

**Ignoring SERP volatility.** Semrush shows you current rankings, but some niches flip constantly. Check the SERP History for any keyword before you invest serious writing time in it. If the top results change every two weeks, ranking there will not hold.

**Using Semrush data without verifying manually.** Semrush keyword volume figures are estimates. Before building an entire content cluster around a keyword, search it on Google yourself. See what is actually ranking, how fresh the results are, and whether there is commercial activity on the page.

**Skipping the content audit step.** New affiliates almost always want to publish more content instead of fixing what they have. This is a mistake. One well-optimized existing article can outperform three new mediocre ones.

**Over-relying on a single competitor.** Analyzing one competitor gives you a narrow view. Run at least three different sites through Keyword Gap. The overlapping opportunities across multiple competitors are almost always the safest bets.

## Pro Tips

**Use Semrush's "Easy to Rank" filter inside Keyword Magic Tool.** It is not perfect, but it surfaces low-difficulty keywords that the standard KD filter sometimes misses. Worth checking on every research session.

**Set up branded keyword tracking for your affiliate programs.** Add the brand names you promote as tracked keywords. When a brand runs a sale or gets press coverage, its search volume spikes and your existing review pages can capture that traffic with no extra work.

**Combine Semrush research with AI writing tools strategically.** I use Semrush to find the keywords and structure, then [Copy.ai](https://copy.ai/referral/chronostrail&utm_source=blog) for drafting product comparison sections quickly. The research comes first, always. Tools like [Jasper](https://jasper.ai/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) are better for longer-form reviews where tone consistency matters.

**Export and date-stamp your keyword research files.** Keyword data changes. What was low difficulty in January might be highly competitive by July. Keeping dated exports lets you spot trends and revisit opportunities at the right time.

**Use the Backlink Gap tool alongside Keyword Gap.** Once you know what content to create, check the Backlink Gap to see which sites are linking to your competitors but not to you. That list becomes your outreach target list for link building after your content is live.
