## 6. Lofty AI , Best for Lead Scoring and CRM Intelligence

Lofty AI (formerly Chime) is purpose-built for real estate, which puts it in a different category than the general AI writing tools above. The lead intelligence features alone save agents hours of manual CRM sorting every week. Instead of guessing who to call, Lofty tells you who's actually ready to buy or sell based on their activity patterns. This is one of the clearest examples of AI doing real work in a real estate workflow.

**Best for:** Lead scoring, automated follow-up, CRM management, and pipeline prioritization.

## 7. ChatGPT (with Real Estate Prompts) , Best for Flexible, Everyday AI Assistance

ChatGPT doesn't have a single real estate function. It has every function, as long as you know how to prompt it correctly. I use it daily for drafting client emails, creating scripts for listing presentations, writing offer letters, and brainstorming marketing angles for unusual properties.

The key is having a library of solid prompts. Agents who plug in generic requests get generic output. Agents who write detailed, specific prompts get material they can actually use with minimal editing. The tool itself costs very little, and the ceiling on its usefulness is almost entirely dependent on how well you use it.

**Best for:** Email drafting, listing presentation scripts, offer letter templates, negotiation talking points, and creative marketing copy.
