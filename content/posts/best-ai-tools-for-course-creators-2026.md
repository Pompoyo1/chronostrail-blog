## 7. Descript

Descript turns video editing into a text editing experience. You upload your recorded lesson, it transcribes everything, and then you edit the video by editing the text transcript. Removing filler words, cutting sections, and adding captions all happen in the same document-style interface.

**Best for:** Editing course video lessons and removing filler words at scale

**Pricing:** Free plan available; Creator plan at $24/month

**Rating:** 4.6/5

If you record your own course videos and spend hours editing them, Descript will cut that time down dramatically. I went from three hours of editing per lesson to under 45 minutes.

## 8. Notion AI

Notion AI is not a standalone AI tool, but if you are already using Notion to organize your course business, the AI layer adds serious value. I use it to brainstorm curriculum structures, draft module summaries, and turn rough voice notes into formatted lesson outlines. The context awareness is strong because the AI sees everything else you have written in your workspace.

**Best for:** Organizing course curriculum, drafting outlines, and managing production workflows

**Pricing:** Notion AI add-on at $10/month per member

**Rating:** 4.3/5

This is not a replacement for a dedicated writing or SEO tool, but as a productivity layer on top of your existing workspace, it is worth every dollar.

## How to Choose the Right Tool for Your Stage

Not every creator needs every tool. Here is how I think about it based on where you are in your business.

**If you are pre-launch and still building your course,** start with Notion AI to organize your curriculum and Writesonic to draft your outlines and module descriptions. Add Semrush if you want to validate your topic with real search data before committing.

**If you are at the launch stage,** Jasper and Copy.ai are the two tools that will do the most work. Jasper handles the long-form sales page and email sequence. Copy.ai automates the promotional workflow so you are not writing every piece manually.

**If you want organic traffic,** Surfer SEO paired with Semrush is the combination I use. Semrush finds the keywords. Surfer tells you how to write content that ranks for them.

**If video is your core format,** Synthesia and Descript solve opposite problems. Synthesia creates video without recording yourself. Descript makes editing recorded video fast enough to actually be worth doing.
