## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### Step 1: Log Into Writesonic and Open the AI Article Writer

Go to your Writesonic dashboard and click "AI Article Writer." In 2026, this is still the most reliable feature for generating structured briefs, not just full drafts. Select "Article Writer 6.0" if available in your account tier.

### Step 2: Enter Your Keyword and Set the Intent

Type your target keyword into the topic field. Then select the search intent from the dropdown. For informational how-to content, choose "Informational." Getting this wrong will give you a brief built around the wrong angle, and that wastes everyone's time.

### Step 3: Configure the Tone and Audience

Set the tone to match your brand. I keep mine on "Informative but conversational" for most client work. Add your audience description in the secondary field. Something like "B2B content marketers who manage freelance writers" works better than "marketers."

### Step 4: Generate the Outline First, Not the Full Article

Click "Generate Outline" rather than jumping straight to a full article. This gives you a skeleton you can edit before Writesonic writes 2,000 words in the wrong direction. You can regenerate the outline 2 to 3 times and pick the best version. This step saves the most time in my workflow.

### Step 5: Edit the Outline Into a Content Brief Format

Take the generated outline and add the following fields directly inside the Writesonic editor or export to Google Docs:

- Target keyword and secondary keywords
- Word count target
- Search intent
- Internal links to include
- Competitor URLs to beat
- Call to action at the end

This turns a content outline into an actual brief. The difference matters when you hand it to a writer.

### Step 6: Run the SEO Check Inside Writesonic

Writesonic has a built-in keyword density and readability check. Use it before you finalize the brief. If important secondary keywords are missing from the suggested headers, add them manually. I also cross-reference this against [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) for competitive content briefs where rankings matter more than speed.

### Step 7: Export and Distribute

Export the brief as a PDF or Google Doc. If you're working with a team, Google Docs is faster for collaboration. If you're solo, a PDF keeps things clean for client delivery. Add any brand guidelines or style notes at the top before sending.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Skipping the intent setting.** This is the single biggest mistake I see. If you set the wrong intent, every section Writesonic generates will be slightly off. Fixing a misaligned brief takes longer than starting over.

**Accepting the first outline without reviewing it.** Writesonic is fast, but the first outline is a draft. Regenerate it at least once and compare. I've gotten dramatically better structures on the second or third attempt.

**Using Writesonic alone for competitive keywords.** For high-stakes content, pair it with a proper SEO tool. [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) gives you data on heading frequency and NLP terms that Writesonic doesn't surface by default.

**Not adding brand context.** Generic briefs produce generic content. If you skip the audience description and tone settings, your writer gets a brief that could belong to any company in your industry.

**Treating the export as the final product.** The brief still needs human review before it goes to a writer. Writesonic is a starting point, not a sign-off step.
