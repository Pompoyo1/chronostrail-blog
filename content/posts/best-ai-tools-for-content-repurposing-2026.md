## What Most People Get Wrong About Content Repurposing

Repurposing is not copying. The biggest mistake I see creators make is feeding a blog post into an AI tool and expecting a great LinkedIn post to come out the other end without any thought. Different platforms have different content languages.

A LinkedIn post that performs has a hook, a personal angle, and white space. A Twitter thread needs escalating stakes in each tweet. An email needs a subject line that competes with 80 other emails in an inbox. The best AI tools for content repurposing understand these differences, but you still need to know what you're asking for.

Use these tools to handle the volume. Keep your judgment engaged for the strategy.
