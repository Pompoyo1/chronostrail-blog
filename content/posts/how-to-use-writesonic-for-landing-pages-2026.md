Your landing page went live last Tuesday. By Friday, you had a 34% conversion rate. That is what happens when you use [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) correctly instead of just poking around hoping the AI figures out what you need.

## What You Need Before Starting

Do not skip this section. Showing up unprepared costs you an hour of bad output and frustration.

**Accounts and tools:**
- A [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) account, Individual or Teams plan. The free tier limits your word count too aggressively to be useful for full landing pages.
- A clear product description, 2 to 5 sentences, written by you before you open the tool.
- Your target audience defined. "Small business owners" is not enough. "E-commerce founders doing $10k to $50k monthly who are losing time on manual inventory tracking" is what you need.
- Your core offer and the single most important benefit.
- Optional but recommended: a [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) account if you want your landing page to rank, not just convert.

**What to have written down before you log in:**
- The problem your product solves
- Three key features
- Three benefits tied to those features
- Social proof you can drop into the copy (testimonials, numbers, client names)

This prep work takes 20 minutes. It will save you 90 minutes of bad AI output.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### Step 1: Log In and Open the Landing Page Generator

Go to your Writesonic dashboard. In the left sidebar, click "Templates." Search for "Landing Page" in the search bar. You will see a few options. Click "Landing Page (Entire Page)." This gives you a full-page output including headline, subheadline, features, benefits, CTA, and social proof sections.

### Step 2: Fill Out the Input Fields Deliberately

This is where most people get lazy and ruin their output. Writesonic gives you fields for product name, product description, and target audience. Do not write one sentence and hit generate.

For the product description field, paste your prepared 2 to 5 sentence description. Be specific about the outcome your product delivers. For target audience, write a full sentence describing your buyer with their pain point included.

### Step 3: Set Your Language and Quality Level

Writesonic has a quality toggle. Set it to "Premium." Yes, it uses more credits. The difference in output quality is significant and worth it every time.

Select your language. If you are writing for US audiences, use English (US). If you are writing for a UK audience or international market, choose accordingly. This affects idioms, spelling, and tone in ways that matter.

### Step 4: Generate and Review the First Output

Hit Generate. You will get a full landing page draft in under 60 seconds. Do not immediately regenerate if you do not love it. Read through the full output first.

I mark three things on the first read: what landed well, what needs rewriting, and what is completely off. Usually 60 to 70 percent of the first output is usable with light editing.

### Step 5: Use the Rewrite Feature on Weak Sections

Highlight any section you do not like. Writesonic lets you rewrite individual blocks. This is faster than regenerating the entire page and losing copy that was working.

For headlines specifically, use the "Content Rephrase" template to generate five to ten variations. Pick the one that leads with the clearest benefit or the most specific outcome. Vague headlines kill conversions.

### Step 6: Optimize Your CTA Copy

The default CTA copy Writesonic generates is often generic. "Get started today" works fine but is not memorable. Use the "Call to Action" template separately to generate 10 CTA variations tied to your specific offer.

The best CTAs I have seen come out of Writesonic are benefit-driven and specific, for example: "Start tracking inventory in 5 minutes" beats "Sign up free" every time.

### Step 7: Run the Copy Through a Readability and SEO Check

Before you publish anything, paste your landing page copy into [Surfer SEO's](https://surferseo.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) Content Editor. This shows you keyword density, readability score, and gaps compared to top-ranking pages on the same topic.

If SEO is not a priority for this page, at minimum paste the copy into Hemingway App. Landing page copy should read at a Grade 6 to 8 level. Dense, complex sentences reduce conversions.

### Step 8: Edit for Brand Voice

Writesonic does not know your brand voice unless you tell it. After generating and selecting your best output, go through the copy manually and adjust the tone to match how your brand actually speaks.

If you use a lot of humor, add it. If your brand is direct and no-nonsense, strip any fluff. This editing pass takes 15 to 20 minutes and makes the copy feel like it came from your team, not a template.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using vague input and expecting specific output.** The AI reflects what you give it. Garbage input produces generic copy that could be for any product in any industry.

**Regenerating everything instead of rewriting sections.** If your headline is solid but your features section is weak, rewrite the features section. Hitting regenerate again risks losing the headline that was working.

**Publishing the first draft without editing.** Writesonic is fast. That speed creates a false sense of "done." Always do at least one manual editing pass. Always.

**Ignoring the CTA entirely.** Marketers spend time on headlines and body copy, then use the first CTA the AI generates. The CTA is the last thing a reader sees before deciding to act. It deserves attention.

**Skipping the audience field.** I have seen people leave the target audience field blank or write "everyone." The output is noticeably worse and more generic. Fill it in with specifics.

## Pro Tips

**Stack tools for better results.** I use [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) to generate the initial draft, then run the final copy through [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) for keyword optimization, and use [SEMrush](https://semrush.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) to validate that I am targeting the right search intent for the page. These tools work better together than any single tool alone.

**Save your best prompts.** When you find a product description input that produces excellent output, save it in a doc. Your future projects will benefit from the structure and specificity of inputs that already worked.

**Test two versions.** Writesonic makes it cheap and fast to generate multiple landing page variations. Generate two, change the headline angle and CTA, then A/B test. You will learn what your audience responds to faster than guessing.

**Use Writesonic for sections, not just full pages.** The full landing page template is great, but individual templates like "Feature to Benefit," "Pain-Agitate-Solution," and "Social Proof" can upgrade specific sections of existing pages. I use these constantly to punch up weak copy on older pages.

**Compare outputs across tools occasionally.** [Jasper](https://jasper.ai/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) and [Copy.ai](https://copy.ai/referral/chronostrail&utm_source=blog) both generate landing page copy. Running the same brief through two tools sometimes surfaces an angle or phrasing Writesonic missed. I do this for high-stakes pages where the conversion rate has direct revenue impact.
