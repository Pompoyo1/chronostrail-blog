## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### Step 1: Run a YouTube Keyword Search Inside Semrush

Open Semrush and go to the Keyword Magic Tool. Type your main topic into the search bar. Before you hit enter, change the database to match your target country.

Once results load, filter by "Questions" in the left sidebar. YouTube searches skew heavily toward question-based queries in 2026, especially with voice and AI-assisted search growing. You want phrases like "how to," "what is," and "best way to."

Sort by search volume, then look at Keyword Difficulty (KD%). Target keywords with 1,000 to 10,000 monthly searches and a KD% under 60. That is your sweet spot for a channel under 50,000 subscribers.

### Step 2: Cross-Reference with Google Video Results

Here is a step most creators skip. Search your target keyword on Google directly. If Google is showing a video carousel or a YouTube embed in the top results, that keyword has video intent. Semrush confirms search volume, but Google confirms whether YouTube videos are actually ranking for that term.

Keywords with strong video intent give you two ranking opportunities at once: YouTube search and Google search. I prioritize these every time. Double the surface area, same video.

### Step 3: Analyze Competitor Channels with Semrush

Go to the Domain Overview tool in Semrush. Type in a competitor's YouTube channel URL (format: youtube.com/c/channelname). Semrush will show you which queries are driving traffic to their content.

Pull the top 20 keywords their videos rank for. Export this to your spreadsheet. Look for keywords they are ranking on page two or lower. Those are gaps you can exploit with better-optimized content.

### Step 4: Build Your Video Title and Description Using Keyword Data

Take your primary keyword and place it in the first five words of your video title. This is not optional. YouTube's algorithm weights front-loaded keywords more heavily than those buried at the end.

Your description should include the primary keyword in the first two sentences, then two to three secondary keywords (pulled from your Semrush list) woven naturally into the next 150 words. Do not stuff. Write for a person, optimize for an algorithm.

For scripting the actual video, I use [Jasper](https://jasper.ai/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) to draft keyword-rich scripts faster, and [Copy.ai](https://copy.ai/referral/chronostrail&utm_source=blog) when I need multiple description variations to test. Both tools let you feed in your target keywords and generate content that holds the keyword density without sounding robotic.

### Step 5: Identify Trending Topics with Semrush Topic Research

Open the Topic Research tool and enter your niche. Switch the view to "Trending" and set the filter to the last 60 days. This shows you what people are actively searching for right now, not six months ago.

Pick one trending subtopic per week and build a video around it. Trending content gets indexed and surfaced faster because YouTube sees rising search demand and pushes relevant videos. Pairing a trending keyword with solid retention metrics is one of the fastest ways to grow in 2026.

### Step 6: Track Your Video Rankings Over Time

This is where most people drop the ball. They optimize once, publish, and forget it.

In Semrush, use the Position Tracking tool. Add your YouTube video URLs as pages to track. Input the keywords you targeted. Check rankings weekly for the first month, then monthly after that.

If a video stalls below position 10 after three weeks, update the description with secondary keywords from your Semrush list. Sometimes that small change is enough to push a video into the top five.

### Step 7: Use the Semrush Backlink Audit for Channel Authority

YouTube channel authority is partly driven by external links pointing to your videos. Go to Backlink Analytics in Semrush and enter a top competitor's channel URL. Look at who is linking to their videos.

Reach out to those same websites. Offer to be a resource, contribute a guest post, or get your video embedded. One strong backlink from a high-authority site can accelerate ranking faster than a dozen low-quality links.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Targeting keywords that are too broad.** "How to lose weight" is not a YouTube keyword strategy. It is a wish. Get specific: "how to lose weight without cardio for beginners" is winnable.

**Ignoring click-through rate signals.** Semrush gives you the keyword data, but YouTube rewards videos that get clicked. A bad thumbnail kills every optimization effort. Test at least two thumbnail designs per video.

**Treating YouTube SEO as a one-time task.** Algorithms shift. Competitors publish. Trends change. Set a monthly calendar reminder to review your tracked keywords in Semrush and refresh underperforming descriptions.

**Skipping tags entirely.** Tags matter less than they did in 2020, but they are not dead. Include your primary keyword and three to five variations in the tag field. Takes two minutes. Do it.

**Relying on auto-generated captions.** Closed captions are indexed. Bad captions hurt comprehension and retention. Upload a clean SRT file. Semrush cannot fix what YouTube cannot read.
