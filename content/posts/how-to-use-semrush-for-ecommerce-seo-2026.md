# How to Use Semrush for Ecommerce SEO in 2026: A Practical Walkthrough

One of my clients went from page 4 to page 1 for three high-converting product keywords in 60 days. No paid ads. No backlink outreach campaign. Just a cleaner keyword strategy and better on-page optimization, built entirely inside [Semrush](https://semrush.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog). This guide shows you exactly how to replicate that process.

---

## What You Need Before Starting

You need an active Semrush account. The free tier is too limited for serious ecommerce work, so I recommend at least the Pro plan ($139.95/month). If you are not ready to commit, use the 7-day free trial to run through this entire walkthrough first.

You also need access to your ecommerce site's backend so you can implement changes as you go. This is not a theoretical exercise. You should be making edits in real time.

Finally, have a list of your top 20 to 30 product or category pages ready. These are your priority targets.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### Step 1: Run a Full Site Audit (20 minutes)

Go to **Site Audit** in the left sidebar and create a new project for your domain. Set the crawl limit to at least 10,000 pages if you have a large catalog.

Once the audit finishes, filter errors by severity. Fix broken links, missing meta descriptions, duplicate title tags, and crawlability issues first. These kill your rankings before you even get to keyword work.

### Step 2: Find Your Real Keyword Gaps (30 minutes)

Go to **Keyword Gap** under the Competitive Research section. Enter your domain and two or three direct competitors. Set the filter to "Missing" keywords.

This shows you every keyword your competitors rank for that you do not. For ecommerce, sort by search volume and filter for commercial intent keywords, anything with "buy", "best", "cheap", or product-specific modifiers. Export this list to a spreadsheet.

### Step 3: Prioritize by Keyword Difficulty and Search Volume (15 minutes)

Inside **Keyword Magic Tool**, paste your top exported keywords one batch at a time. Filter by KD% under 50 and search volume above 500. These are your low-hanging fruit.

I focus on keywords with KD between 20 and 45 first. High volume with low difficulty means faster wins, which builds authority you can use to attack harder terms later.

### Step 4: Map Keywords to Specific Pages (20 minutes)

Open a spreadsheet with two columns: keyword and target URL. Every keyword needs one destination. Never assign two keywords to the same page unless they are tightly related variations.

Category pages should target broader terms. Product pages should target specific, high-intent terms. If you do not have a page for a keyword cluster, flag it as a content gap and create one.

### Step 5: Optimize On-Page Elements with Position Tracking (25 minutes)

Set up **Position Tracking** in Semrush and add your priority keywords. This becomes your baseline. Then go to **On Page SEO Checker** and run it against your target pages.

The tool gives you specific recommendations for title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, and internal links. I implement these changes page by page, starting with the highest-traffic pages. Do not skip the internal linking suggestions. They are consistently undervalued.

### Step 6: Analyze Competitor Content for Content Gaps (20 minutes)

Go to **Organic Research** and enter a competitor's domain. Click on their top pages sorted by traffic. Look for product guides, comparison posts, or category-level content that ranks well.

These are content types you are likely missing. For each gap, create a content brief. I use [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) to build those briefs and pair it with [Jasper](https://jasper.ai/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) or [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) to produce the actual content faster without sacrificing quality.

### Step 7: Build a Backlink Acquisition List (30 minutes)

Go to **Backlink Gap** and compare your domain against competitors. Filter for high-authority domains (Authority Score above 40) that link to multiple competitors but not to you. These are your warmest outreach targets.

Export the list and prioritize by Authority Score. These sites already understand your niche and link to similar content. Your outreach conversion rate will be much higher than cold pitching.

### Step 8: Set Up Monthly Reporting (10 minutes)

In **My Reports**, create a custom report that pulls Position Tracking data, organic traffic trends from the Traffic Analytics module, and Site Audit health scores. Schedule it to send automatically each month.

This keeps you accountable and makes it easy to spot when a ranking drops before it becomes a traffic problem. Catching a drop early is the difference between a quick fix and a full recovery project.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Targeting keywords that are too broad.** "Running shoes" is not a realistic target for a mid-size ecommerce store. "Men's trail running shoes under $100" is. Specificity wins in ecommerce SEO.

**Ignoring your site audit results.** I see this constantly. People skip straight to keyword research while their site is riddled with 404 errors and duplicate content. Technical issues cap your ceiling regardless of how good your content is.

**Treating every page equally.** Your top 10 revenue-generating pages deserve 80% of your optimization effort. Spreading attention evenly across 500 product pages is a losing strategy.

**Not tracking changes.** If you do not record when you made an on-page change, you cannot connect the action to the result. Use Position Tracking to log baselines before any edit goes live.

**Relying on Semrush data in isolation.** Semrush is excellent, but I cross-reference keyword data with Google Search Console to validate real impressions and click-through rates. Both tools together give a clearer picture than either one alone.

## Pro Tips

**Use the Semrush Writing Assistant for product descriptions.** It is inside the Content Marketing toolkit and checks your copy against your target keyword in real time. Pair it with [Copy.ai](https://copy.ai/referral/chronostrail&utm_source=blog) to generate description variations quickly, then refine in the Writing Assistant.

**Sort Keyword Gap results by CPC, not just volume.** High CPC keywords signal commercial intent. Advertisers are bidding on those terms because buyers are searching them. That is exactly where you want to rank organically.

**Check your cannibalization report monthly.** Under Position Tracking, Semrush flags when multiple pages compete for the same keyword. Cannibalization quietly destroys rankings, and most store owners never notice it until a traffic cliff appears in their analytics.

**Run competitor audits before writing any new content.** Knowing the exact word count, heading structure, and topic coverage of the top-ranking page tells you the minimum bar you need to clear. Guessing wastes time and budget.

**Use the Topic Research tool for seasonal content planning.** Ecommerce SEO is cyclical. Black Friday content needs to be indexed months in advance. Topic Research shows trending subtopics so you can time your content calendar to capture seasonal traffic before competitors do.
