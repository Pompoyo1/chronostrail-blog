# How to Use Copy.ai for Landing Pages in 2026: Boost Conversions

Your landing page rewrites in 20 minutes. Conversion rate jumps from 1.8% to 4.3%. That is what happened when I stopped writing landing page copy from scratch and started using [Copy.ai](https://copy.ai/referral/chronostrail&utm_source=blog) as my first draft engine.

This is not about replacing your thinking. It is about removing the blank page problem and getting to a working draft fast enough to test, iterate, and actually ship.

Here is exactly how to do it.

## What You Need Before Starting

You do not need to be a copywriter. You do need a few things in place before you open Copy.ai.

**Accounts and tools:**

- A [Copy.ai](https://copy.ai/referral/chronostrail&utm_source=blog) account (the free tier works for testing, but Pro gives you unlimited generations and workflow access)
- A clear product or offer defined in one sentence
- Your target audience written out: who they are, what they want, and what pain they are trying to escape
- Basic keyword research done ahead of time (I use [Semrush](https://semrush.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) for this)

If you skip the audience and keyword research, Copy.ai will generate generic copy that sounds fine but converts poorly. Garbage in, garbage out.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### Step 1: Set Up Your Brand Voice (5 minutes)

Log into Copy.ai and go to **Brand Voice** under your workspace settings. Paste three to five samples of copy you like, whether that is your own past work or a competitor whose tone resonates with your audience.

Copy.ai will analyze the samples and create a saved voice profile. Every output you generate will now match that tone instead of defaulting to corporate filler.

### Step 2: Use the Landing Page Workflow (10 minutes)

Click **Workflows** in the left sidebar. Search for "Landing Page" and select the pre-built landing page generator.

You will be prompted to enter your product name, your value proposition, your target customer, and the main pain point you solve. Be specific here. "Small business owners who lose leads because their website copy is boring" outperforms "business owners" every time.

### Step 3: Generate Your Above-the-Fold Copy First (5 minutes)

Inside the workflow, run the headline and subheadline section first. Copy.ai will give you five to ten variations.

Do not pick the first one that sounds good. Read them all, then look for the one that names the outcome most clearly. The best headline I have gotten from Copy.ai was not the flashiest. It was the most direct.

### Step 4: Generate Features and Benefits Blocks (8 minutes)

Move to the features section of the workflow. Paste in your product features as bullet points, and let Copy.ai convert them into benefit-led copy.

This is where [Copy.ai](https://copy.ai/referral/chronostrail&utm_source=blog) genuinely saves time. Translating features into benefits is the part most people struggle with. The tool does it quickly and usually gets 70 to 80 percent of the way there on the first pass.

### Step 5: Write the Social Proof Section Manually (10 minutes)

Do not use AI to fabricate testimonials or case study snippets. Pull your real testimonials and drop them into the page structure Copy.ai built.

If you do not have testimonials yet, use the Copy.ai testimonial template to draft a request email you can send to existing customers. Real quotes, even short ones, outperform the best AI-generated social proof every time.

### Step 6: Generate Your CTA Copy (5 minutes)

Return to the workflow and run the CTA section. Enter the action you want visitors to take and the primary objection they might have before taking it.

Copy.ai will generate button copy and surrounding text that addresses the objection directly. I have found this alone lifts click-through rates because most CTA copy ignores the hesitation sitting right above the button.

### Step 7: Run an SEO Check Before Publishing (15 minutes)

Copy.ai does not optimize for search by default. Once your draft is done, run it through [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) to check keyword density, heading structure, and content score.

Surfer will flag missing terms and structural gaps that Copy.ai misses. This step is what separates landing pages that rank from ones that just sit there looking nice.

### Step 8: Edit, Cut, and Tighten (20 minutes)

Read your page out loud. Cut any sentence that a real person would not say in a conversation.

Copy.ai tends to over-explain in places. Your job in editing is to remove that. The goal is a page where every sentence earns its place.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using the output without editing it.** Copy.ai gives you a draft, not a finished page. Brands that publish raw AI output have a recognizable sameness that readers notice and distrust.

**Skipping the brand voice setup.** Without it, every page you generate sounds like every other page using the tool. Your brand voice is a competitive advantage. Feed it into the tool properly.

**Treating every headline variant as equal.** The tool generates options, not rankings. You still need judgment to pick the headline that actually names what your customer wants. If you struggle with this, [Jasper](https://jasper.ai/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) has a slightly different approach to headline generation that some people find produces stronger options for specific niches.

**Ignoring the mobile version.** Copy that works on desktop often reads as too long on mobile. After your Surfer check, paste your copy into a mobile preview and cut ruthlessly.

**Not testing variants.** Copy.ai makes it cheap to generate multiple headline versions. There is no reason to run a single version of your page without an A/B test set up. Generate three headline variants and test them.

## Pro Tips

**Use Chat mode for the tricky sections.** The workflow covers the standard blocks well, but for unusual sections like comparison tables, pricing explanations, or FAQ copy, switch to Copy.ai Chat. You can have a back-and-forth that gets more nuanced output than a single workflow run.

**Combine tools deliberately.** I use [Semrush](https://semrush.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) to find my target keywords before I open Copy.ai, then use [Surfer](https://surferseo.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) after to optimize the output. Copy.ai sits in the middle as the writing engine. Treating it as the only tool you need is how you end up with pages that look great but get no traffic.

**Save your best outputs as templates.** Once you have a structure that converts, save it in your Copy.ai workspace. Future pages get faster and more consistent because you are iterating on something that already works rather than starting fresh every time.

**Use Writesonic as a comparison check.** I occasionally run the same brief through [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog) alongside Copy.ai to see which tool produces stronger hooks for a particular offer. They approach copy generation differently, and for certain industries one tool consistently beats the other. Testing costs you ten minutes and might save you a conversion problem you would not have caught otherwise.

**Do not let the tool set your pricing copy.** Copy.ai can write around your pricing, but it should never write the actual framing of your price. That requires knowing your market position, your competitor pricing, and your customer psychology. Write that section yourself.
