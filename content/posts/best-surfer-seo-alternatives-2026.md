## The 6 Best Surfer SEO Alternatives

### 1. Semrush Content Marketing Toolkit

Semrush is the most complete platform on this list, and it is not close. Beyond keyword research and backlink analysis, its SEO Writing Assistant and Content Audit tools give you real-time optimization scores, readability feedback, and tone analysis inside a single dashboard. I've used this on agency retainers where clients needed both technical SEO and content output, and it handled both without requiring a second tool.

**Best for:** Teams that need an all-in-one SEO platform with content optimization built in.

[Try Semrush here](https://semrush.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog)

### 2. Jasper AI

Jasper is the tool I reach for when the brief is solid but the writing is stalling. It uses your brand voice settings and integrates with Surfer SEO natively, which means you can pull in keyword targets and let Jasper draft content against them without switching tabs. The output quality is consistently above what I get from generic AI tools, especially for long-form blog posts and sales pages.

**Best for:** Content marketers who want fast, brand-consistent drafts optimized around target keywords.

[Try Jasper here](https://jasper.ai/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog)

---

### 3. Copy.ai

Copy.ai has evolved well past its "AI copywriting gimmick" phase. The Workflows feature lets you build multi-step content pipelines, so you can go from keyword input to a full optimized draft with SEO metadata in one automated sequence. For teams producing high content volume, that kind of repeatability saves real hours every week.

**Best for:** Marketing teams running high-volume content operations who need automation without sacrificing output quality.

[Try Copy.ai here](https://copy.ai/referral/chronostrail&utm_source=blog)

---

### 4. Writesonic

Writesonic is the best option on this list if budget is a constraint but you still need solid SEO-focused content output. Its Chatsonic feature adds live web access to the AI, which means it can pull current data and trends into your drafts rather than relying on a frozen training set. The SEO mode generates articles with keyword density checks, meta descriptions, and heading structure baked into the workflow.

**Best for:** Solo creators and small teams that want capable AI writing with built-in SEO features at a lower price point.

[Try Writesonic here](https://writesonic.com/?via=chronostrail&utm_source=blog)

---

### 5. Clearscope

Clearscope is the tool I trust most for pure content optimization scoring. It analyzes top-ranking pages for your target keyword and returns a clean list of terms and topics your content needs to cover to be competitive. The grading system is straightforward, the interface is quiet and focused, and it plugs directly into Google Docs and WordPress so editors can optimize without leaving their writing environment.

**Best for:** Editors and SEO managers who want precise content grading without a bloated feature set getting in the way.

*Note: Clearscope does not offer an affiliate program, but you can explore their plans directly at clearscope.io.*

---

### 6. MarketMuse

MarketMuse goes deeper on content strategy than anything else on this list. It maps your entire site's topic authority, identifies content gaps, and prioritizes which pages to build or update based on your competitive position. The AI-generated content briefs are detailed enough that a writer can produce a fully optimized draft without additional research, which is rare.

**Best for:** Content strategists and SEO leads at growing companies who need a prioritization system, not just a writing assistant.

*Note: MarketMuse does not offer an affiliate program. Visit marketmuse.com to explore pricing and demos.*
